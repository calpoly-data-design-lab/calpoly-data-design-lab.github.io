# About

The Cal Poly Data Design Lab is an academic research group at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo.  

## What we study

Our work covers a wide range of topics within data science, data visualization, and human-computer interaction. Our core mission is to further the study of human-centered approaches to data science, and support the community of scholars interested in these areas at Cal Poly.
