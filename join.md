# Join the lab

We welcome any students and collaborators interested in human-centered data science, data visualization, and human-computer interaction.

## Students

Students can get involved through independent study, senior projects, thesis work, course-based research, or paid research assistant positions when funding is available.

To inquire, email [{{ site.data.site_settings.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site_settings.email }}) with:

1. A short note about your interests.
2. Relevant coursework, projects, or research experience.
3. The projects or research themes you are most excited about.
4. Your availability and preferred timeline.

## Collaborators

Faculty, community organizations, and industry partners are invited to contact us about research collaborations involving data, visualization, interaction design, or public-interest technology.
